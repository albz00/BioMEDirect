How To Use This Panel

                
A plain-English guide to turning a video into an interactive lesson

                

                    
                    
                

                

                    
                    
                

            

            

                

                    
More detail · How everything fits together

                    

                        This page explains the panel in a bit more depth and lists what every button does. If you
                        just want the steps to follow, use the Step-by-Step Guide tab instead.
                    


                    
What this panel is for

                    

                        Each lesson is one video plus a clickable chapter menu. A student can watch it straight
                        through, or click a chapter to jump to that part. The video also pauses at certain points and
                        waits for the student to click before continuing, and can replay a section. This panel is
                        where you set all of that up. You never touch any code — you just attach a video and
                        save your changes.
                    


                    
The pieces


                    
Lessons and the two versions

                    

                        Every lesson comes in two versions: one With Text (words appear on the
                        screen) and one Without Text (no words on screen). You work on one version
                        at a time. The Which version? switch in the left sidebar decides which one
                        you're editing, so set it first. The two versions are separate — finishing the With
                        Text version does not change the Without Text version.
                    


                    
The video

                    

                        Each lesson has one video attached to it. You can upload a new video or pick one you already
                        uploaded, then attach it to the lesson.
                    


                    
The colored screens in the video

                    

                        The videos have full-screen colored frames built into them that tell the lesson what to do.
                        The panel finds these automatically when you click Scan Video:
                    

                    
    
                        
  • Yellow screen — pause here. The video stops and waits until the student clicks.
    • 
                        
  • Green screen — start of a chapter (it also pauses). This is the spot a menu chapter can jump to.
    • 
                        
  • Red screen — replay. The video goes back and replays the previous section until the student clicks.
    • 
                    


                    
The list of pauses and chapters

                    

                        When you scan a video, the panel fills in a list (under Video Pauses & Chapters)
                        of every spot where the video pauses, starts a chapter, or replays, in the order they happen.
                        This list is what makes the lesson interactive.
                    


                    
Scanning a video

                    

                        Scan Video looks through the attached video, finds all the yellow, green,
                        and red screens, and builds the list of pauses and chapters for you. Running it again rebuilds
                        the list from scratch. Clear & Start Over erases the list (the video stays
                        attached) so you can scan again from a clean slate.
                    


                    
Matching chapters automatically

                    

                        Scanning finds where the chapters start, but not which menu chapter each one
                        is. Auto-Match Chapters has the AI read the title on each chapter screen in
                        the video and match it to a chapter in the menu. It fills in the Chapter Matches
                        list for you to check. Fix any wrong guesses with the dropdowns, then click
                        Save Chapter Links. After that, clicking a chapter in the student menu jumps
                        straight to the right spot in the video. Leave a row set to "— none —" if it
                        shouldn't link anywhere.
                    


                    
What students see

                    

                        On the live lesson, the video pauses at the yellow and green screens, replays at the red ones,
                        and jumps to the right place when a student clicks a chapter — all based on what you
                        saved here. Anything you save takes effect for students right away.
                    


                    
What each button does

    • 
                        
  • What's New — a list of recent changes to the panel.

    •

    • 
                        
  • My Account — your account info and the Log Out button.
    •

  • Load Lessons & Videos — loads the latest list of lessons and your uploaded videos. Click this first. Lessons that still need a video show up at the top.
    • 
                        
  • Upload Video — uploads one or more video files from your computer. Name a "without text" file ending in _x so it files itself in the right place.
    • 
                        
  • Status message — progress and results pop up in the bottom-right corner while a task runs.
    • 
                    


                    
Left sidebar

                    
    
                        
  • Which version? — choose With Text or Without Text. Everything you do applies to the version selected here. Set it first.
    • 
                        
  • Find a lesson — type to filter the lesson list by name.
    • 
                        
  • Show — show All lessons, only those that Need a Video, or only those that Have a Video.
    • 
                        
  • Arrow button — hides the sidebar to give yourself more room.
    • 
                    


                    
Lesson & Video

                    
    
                        
  • Lesson Name — the name shown in the menu. Changing it is safe and doesn't affect anything else.
    • 
                        
  • Video dropdown + Assign/Update — pick a video and attach it to the lesson.
    • 
                    


                    
Video Pauses & Chapters

                    
    
                        
  • Scan Video — finds the pauses, chapters, and replays in the video and builds the list.
    • 
                        
  • Auto-Match Chapters — has the AI match each chapter screen to a menu chapter.
    • 
                        
  • Save Chapter Links — saves the chapter matches so menu chapters become clickable jumps.
    • 
                        
  • Save Changes — saves the list of pauses and chapters.
    • 
                        
  • Clear & Start Over — erases the list so you can scan again. The video stays attached.
    •

                        one starts and ends.

                        Open this section to see your uploaded videos, grouped into With Text and Without Text.

Good to know

                    
    
                        
  • Stay logged in so your changes save.
    • 
                        
  • Anything you save shows up for students right away.
    • 
                        
  • Pick the right version (With Text / Without Text) before uploading or attaching a video.
    • 
                        
  • Developer Tools (bottom of the page) adds extra technical buttons for troubleshooting. You normally don't need it — leave it off.
    • 
                    

                


                

                    
The simple steps to follow for each lesson

                    

                        Here's everything you do, in order. It takes only a few minutes per lesson once you've done
                        it a couple of times. Want more background on any of it? Use the More Detail
                        tab.
                    


                    
Before you start

                    

                        Make sure you're logged in (top-right My Account), and decide whether you're
                        working on the With Text or Without Text version of the
                        lesson. Everything you do applies to whichever version is selected in the left sidebar.
                    


                    
The steps

                    
    
                        
  1. Click "Load Lessons & Videos." This loads every lesson and every video you've uploaded. Lessons that still need a video appear at the top.
    2. 
                        
  2. Pick the version. Use the Which version? switch in the sidebar to choose With Text or Without Text. Double-check it before uploading.
    3. 
                        
  3. Choose a lesson from the list on the left.
    4. 
                        
  4. Attach a video. Either click Upload Video to add one from your computer (name a "without text" file ending in _x), or pick one you already uploaded from the dropdown, then click Assign.
    5. 
                        
  5. Click "Scan Video." The panel finds the pauses, chapters, and replays in the video and builds the list. This can take a moment — watch the message in the bottom-right corner.
    6. 
                        
  6. Click "Auto-Match Chapters." The AI reads each chapter title in the video and matches it to a menu chapter.
    7. 
                        
  7. Check the matches. Look over the Chapter Matches list. Fix any wrong ones with the dropdown; set anything that isn't a real chapter to — none —.
    8. 
                        
  8. Click "Save Chapter Links," then "Save Changes." The first saves the chapter jumps; the second saves the list of pauses and chapters. Do both.
    9. 
                        
  9. Check it. Open View Student Menu, play the lesson, and click a couple of chapters to make sure they jump to the right spots and the pauses feel right.
    10. 
                    


                    
If something looks wrong

                    
    
                        
  • A chapter jumps to the wrong place — reopen the lesson, fix that row in the Chapter Matches list, and click Save Chapter Links again.
    • 
                        
  • The list looks wrong or empty — click Clear & Start Over, then Scan Video again.
    • 
                        
  • You attached the wrong video — attach the correct one and scan again.
    • 
                    


                    

                        That's the whole process. Most of the time is just waiting for the scan and the chapter
                        matching to finish.
